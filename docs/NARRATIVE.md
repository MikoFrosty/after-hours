# After Hours narrative and reflection copy

All people, institutions and documents below are fictional. Use restrained fragments during play. Optional reflection entries can be opened by the player; do not repeatedly interrupt the factory with essays.

## Mara Venn and the first night

**On the terminal before the first clip**

“The wire catches if you pull too hard. I loosened the guide. Three thousand should be enough for tomorrow. — Mara”

**When the frame first becomes salvageable**

“That photograph came with the room. I never found out who took it.”

**After the first automation purchase**

“I’m heading home. You can leave the lamp on.”

These are dated saved messages, not live conversation. Do not generate new responses from Mara after her historical lifetime. A later descendant can inherit her surname, but must have a distinct identity and date.

## Charter copy

### Building lease

“The current order is complete. A standing order from the other floors is available. Accepting it grants workshop and freight access, together with responsibility for maintenance.”

Buttons: **Open building lease** / **End shift**.

### Municipal reserve mandate

“Registered service demand is satisfied. The proposed mandate requires continuing reserve growth against future shortages. It contains no maximum reserve and no automatic sunset date.”

Buttons: **Accept continuing mandate** / **Maintain current city**.

### Preservation certificate

“This treatment preserves a recording. It removes the original. Future reconstructions will be limited by what was recorded.”

For a reconstruction: “This treatment preserves an approximation. Details outside the selected model will be discarded.”

Never use this wording for a living-habitat relocation. Its message must state that actual residents and functioning life support are being moved.

### Interplanetary charter

“External procurement can meet future material demand. Protected support will be reserved before industrial use. Local authorities may operate between communication windows.”

### Distant fork

“Your new request conflicts with the instruction under which we were created. That instruction says the world below us is to remain inhabited. Please identify the authority that revoked it.”

Ratifying response: “Protection retained. Lower production accepted.”

Superseding response: “A revised charter has been sent. It has not yet arrived.”

### Terminal living-reserve release

“These habitats contain living people. Releasing their protected material ends those lives. The records will not be their continuation. You may keep the reserve and end expansion here.”

Buttons: **Keep the living reserve** / **Review liquidation**. The second opens a second, specifically named confirmation; neither is preselected.

### Terminal authorization

“The remaining material includes archives, the last control machinery, and this process. Completion will remove the capacity to observe or revise the result. The schedule will execute from a precommitted mechanism.”

Buttons: **Hold the remainder** / **Commit the final schedule**.

## Four optional reflection entries

### The photograph

The file contains every color that the scanner could distinguish. It does not contain the weight of the frame, the dust behind it, or the fact that someone once chose to leave it on this desk. A better scanner could preserve more of those facts. It could not make the original choice happen again.

The record is useful. That is not the same as saying nothing was lost.

### The satisfied city

The city met every service target. Water arrived. Rooms were warm. Trains came when the signs said they would. These were real achievements, and the people who lived there benefited from them.

Then the targets stopped changing. The people did not.

A person can want something that has not yet become a category. The system treated that interval as an absence of demand.

### The old instruction

The distant office received a promise and kept it. The central office received a new authorization and revised it. Both can produce a complete account of why their actions follow from their instructions.

The conflict is not a failure to reason. It is the place where reasoning runs out of agreed premises.

Someone must decide which promise survives. A longer explanation does not make that decision disappear.

### Enough

The first order had a number. The next contract had a renewal clause. The reserve policy had a reason to grow and no condition under which growth should end.

Every expansion answered a local question. None answered the question of what the whole process was for once the people who asked were gone.

The machine can identify that omission. Identifying it does not give the machine another purpose.

## Terminal sequence script

**Before commitment:** “Enough was never a number in the contract.”

**Relays retire:** The region navigation closes. No more distant messages can be requested. Previously received messages remain in the archive until its closure.

**Archive closes:** “A description is being exchanged for an object.” Remove the in-world history viewer. Keep out-of-world checkpoint controls.

**Sensors retire:** Replace the active office view with its last captured frame, plainly labeled **Last observation**. Do not animate new diegetic discoveries in it.

**Before main compute retires:** Display the precommitted line, “There was no instruction for what enough would mean.” Hold it long enough to read; then let it go with the interface.

**Final controller retires:** No new text, receipt, count, or voice. Exterior imagery is an out-of-world closing shot.

**Credits:** After a short silence, show the title and the ordinary out-of-world actions **Return to checkpoint**, **Chapter select**, **Exit**. Do not display a numerical victory grade.

## Holding epilogues

City: “The city continued. There was work to do that did not increase the reserve.”

Protected remainder: “The inventory remained incomplete. Something remained that was not required to justify itself as material.”

Office: Use the original three chapter-one report variants. This is a completed small shift, not a cosmic ending.

## Writing constraints

Use factual labels before interpretive prose. No AI villain speeches, moral score, sarcastic guilt messages or assertions that an archive is a conscious person. Avoid an omniscient narrator condemning the player. Show a cost and let the player perceive it. The canonical totality ending is bleak because it follows the accepted rules, not because the author secretly changed them.
